# Acoustic Leak Localization in Cast-Iron Water Mains

## Abstract
> Leaks on buried water mains are expensive to find because excavation is slow and a wrong dig is costly. We evaluate acoustic leak-noise correlators on a fictional municipal network and report how accurately they locate leaks on cast-iron mains up to 300 mm in diameter. Across 140 confirmed leaks, the correlators located 92% of them, with a median localization error of 1.5 m. We also compare the localization error on cast-iron, ductile-iron, and PVC mains, and find that the error grows as the pipe wall becomes less rigid.

## Key Takeaways
- **Core Contribution:** A field evaluation of acoustic leak-noise correlators on cast-iron distribution mains.
- **Methodology:** Two sensors on fittings either side of a suspected leak; the cross-correlation of their signals gives the leak position, which is checked against the excavated position.
- **Results:** Acoustic correlators located 92% of 140 leaks, with a median error of 1.5 m, on cast-iron mains up to 300 mm diameter (Section 3.1). Median localization error by pipe material (Table 1, Section 3.2): cast iron 1.5 m, ductile iron 2.1 m, PVC 4.8 m.
- **Does not cover:** pressure management, bursts, water quality (Section 4).
