# Valve Exercising Schedules and Seized Valves

## Abstract
> Isolation valves that are never operated seize, and a seized valve delays the isolation of a main for repair. We compare the share of seized valves on a fictional network before and after a programme that exercised every isolation valve every two years. Exercising valves every two years cut the share of seized valves from 18% to 6%.

## Key Takeaways
- **Core Contribution:** An evaluation of a two-year valve-exercising programme.
- **Methodology:** Every isolation valve on the network was operated through its full travel once every two years; the share of valves found seized was compared before and after the programme.
- **Results:** Seized valves delay the isolation of mains for repair. Exercising valves every two years cut the share of seized valves from 18% to 6%.
