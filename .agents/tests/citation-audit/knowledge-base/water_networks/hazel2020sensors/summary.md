# Low-Cost Pressure Sensors for Transient Monitoring

## Abstract
> Pressure transients in water mains last a fraction of a second, which is far shorter than the sampling interval of a typical network pressure logger. We built low-cost pressure sensors that sample at 100 Hz and installed them beside standard 1 Hz loggers at eight sites of a fictional network. The 100 Hz sensors captured pressure transients above 5 m head that the 1 Hz loggers missed. Each sensor cost a small fraction of a commercial transient logger. We describe the hardware, the side-by-side trial, and the transient events recorded.

## Key Takeaways
- **Core Contribution:** A low-cost 100 Hz pressure sensor for capturing transients in water mains.
- **Methodology:** Sensors costing under one tenth of a commercial transient logger were installed on the same tappings as existing 1 Hz loggers at eight sites for twelve weeks.
- **Results:** Sensors sampling at 100 Hz captured pressure transients above 5 m head that 1 Hz loggers missed (Section 3.1): 37 such transients, 31 of them invisible in the 1 Hz record.
- **Does not cover:** bursts, metering (Section 4).
