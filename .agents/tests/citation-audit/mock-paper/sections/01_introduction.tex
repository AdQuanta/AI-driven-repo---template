\section{Introduction}

Utilities lose a large share of treated water to leaks. Acoustic correlators
can locate leaks on cast-iron mains to within a couple of metres~\cite{alder2021leakage}.

Two operational levers reduce losses further: lowering night-time pressure and
watching district inlet flows for anomalies~\cite{birch2019pressure,cedar2020smartmeters}.

Cold weather matters as well: burst rates in iron mains more than double when
the soil temperature drops below 2~$^\circ$C~\cite{dogwood2018chlorine}.

Seized valves slow every repair, and regular exercising keeps them
working~\cite{fir2017valves}.

Pressure transients, which slow loggers miss, can now be captured with
inexpensive high-rate sensors~\cite{hazel2019sensors}.

Finally, tiered tariffs shift demand away from the daily peak~\cite{ivy2021tariffs}.
