\begin{abstract}
Low-cost digital temperature sensors are cheap enough to deploy in large
numbers, but each unit carries its own offset and gain error. This note
describes a short calibration pipeline that compares every sensor with a
reference thermometer at a few set points, fits a linear correction by least
squares, and reports the residual uncertainty of the corrected readings.
